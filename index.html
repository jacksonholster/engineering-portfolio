Completed · Aug. 2026

DigiCam — ESP32 Digital Camera

ESP32-S3 · KiCad · Onshape · C/C++ · 3D Printing

Designed and built a custom handheld digital camera around the Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense. The project combines embedded programming, electrical design, a live TFT display, physical controls, battery power, flash circuitry, and a custom 3D-printed enclosure.

  • Created multi-sheet schematics for power, display, controls, MCU pin mapping, and MOSFET-driven flash circuitry.
  • Modeled the camera enclosure and custom control components in Onshape and fabricated them with FDM 3D printing.
  • Integrated and debugged the hardware until live preview and image capture worked on the finished prototype.
