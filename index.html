DigiCam

DigiCam is a handheld digital camera centered around a Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense. The project combines embedded programming, electrical design, physical controls, a 2.4-inch SPI TFT, battery power, custom flash circuitry, and a fully custom 3D-printed enclosure. I designed the electronics in KiCad, modeled the mechanical parts in Onshape, assembled the hardware, and debugged the system until the live feed and image capture worked on the finished prototype.

02Electrical

KICAD

Designed as connected subsystems.

The schematic separates the MCU, power system, display, user controls, and MOSFET-driven flash so each part could be understood and debugged independently.

The ESP32-S3 handles SPI display communication, rotary encoder inputs, shutter input, backlight control, and flash control. The design also accounts for the XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense microSD connection.

03CAD

ONSHAPE

Designed around the electronics.

The enclosure was modeled to package the display, camera, controls, battery, and internal wiring into a printable handheld form.

The custom body includes lens and flash openings, control placement, a curved grip, and internal space for the electronics.

04Build

PROTOTYPING

Turning the design into a working object.

Assembly exposed the practical challenges that do not appear in a schematic: wiring, clearances, control fit, connector placement, and debugging.

Hardware integration

The electronics were assembled and integrated into the 3D-printed enclosure, with the display, ESP32-S3, battery, perfboard wiring, and controls packaged into the final camera body.

Working result

The completed prototype runs a live camera preview and captures images using the ESP32-S3 camera system.

Guitar Distortion Pedal

I'm currently designing an analog guitar distortion pedal around an OPA2134PA dual audio op-amp. The project includes gain and clipping stages, tone and volume controls, 9 V power, breadboard validation, and a custom PCB layout.

The circuit is being tested and refined on a breadboard before the PCB is fabricated, with a focus on reliable audio performance, clean routing, and packaging the final electronics into a usable pedal.

  • OPA2134PA dual audio op-amp signal path
  • Diode clipping for distortion generation
  • Gain, tone, and volume control stages
  • 9 V power and reference-voltage design
  • Breadboard testing, troubleshooting, and PCB layout
