DigiCam

A custom ESP32-S3 digital camera built from the electronics outward.

Functional prototype

DigiCam is a handheld digital camera centered around a Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense. The project combines embedded programming, electrical design, physical controls, a 2.4-inch SPI TFT, battery power, custom flash circuitry, and a fully custom 3D-printed enclosure.

The project was an exercise in full-system integration: designing the electronics in KiCad, creating the mechanical components in Onshape, assembling the prototype, and debugging the hardware until a live camera feed and image capture were working on the finished device.

02

Electrical Design

KICAD

Breaking the camera into subsystems.

I organized the electrical design into functional blocks so the MCU, power system, display, controls, and flash circuitry could be developed and debugged independently.

A
System architecture

The ESP32-S3 handles the display SPI signals, encoder inputs, shutter input, backlight control, and flash gate. The schematic set also reserves the built-in microSD connection on the XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense.

03

Mechanical Design

ONSHAPE

Designed around the electronics.

The enclosure and control components were modeled to package the display, camera, controls, and internal wiring into a handheld form that could be fabricated on an FDM printer.

ENCLOSURE

Custom camera body

The body includes a lens opening, front flash opening, curved grip geometry, control placement, and internal space for the electronics.

04

Build & Integration

PROTOTYPING

Turning CAD and schematics into a working object.

The hardware was assembled on perfboard and integrated into the printed enclosure. The build phase exposed the practical problems that don't appear in a schematic: wiring, clearances, connector placement, control fit, and debugging under real constraints.
